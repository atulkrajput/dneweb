Effective date: October 29, 2026

DNE Consultants ("DNE Consultants", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. It applies to:

  • our website at dneco.io (the "Website");
  • mobile applications that we publish under the DNE Consultants or DNECO developer name on Google Play and other app stores (the "Apps"); and
  • related online services, client portals and communications (together with the Website and Apps, the "Services").

This is one shared policy for all of our Apps. Many Apps collect far less than the maximum described below — some collect nothing at all. Section 11, Our Apps lists exactly what each App collects.

Apps that we build for our clients and that are published under a client's own developer account are covered by that client's privacy policy, not this one.

1. Information We Collect

Information you give us

  • Contact and enquiry details – your name, email address, mobile number, company name, the service you are interested in and any message you send us through a contact form, product interest form, email or lead form (including lead forms on Facebook and Instagram).
  • Account details – if an App or client portal lets you create an account: your name, email address, password (stored only in hashed form), profile photo and preferences.
  • Content you provide – files, photos, documents, comments, tasks or other content you choose to upload or enter in the Services.
  • Support communications – information you share when you contact us for help.
  • AI chat messages – the messages you type into the AI chat assistant on our Website, and any details you choose to share in them (such as your name, email address or phone number). If you share an email address, we save the conversation as an enquiry so our team can follow up.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and log information – IP address, browser or device type, operating system and version, app version, language, time zone and the date and time of your requests.
  • Usage information – pages or screens viewed, features used, products viewed, the page you came from (referrer) and campaign parameters such as UTM tags or Google click IDs.
  • Diagnostics – crash reports and performance data that help us find and fix problems in the Apps.
  • Identifiers – cookies and similar technologies on the Website, and on mobile devices an app instance ID or, only where you allow it, an advertising ID.

Information from device permissions (Apps only)

An App will only ask for a device permission when a feature needs it, and only after asking you. Depending on the App, this may include:

  • Camera, photos and files – to take or upload a photo or document you choose.
  • Location – to provide location-based features, only while you use them.
  • Notifications – to send you updates, reminders and alerts.
  • Microphone – for voice input or recording features you start.

You can deny or withdraw any permission at any time in your device settings. Some features may not work without the related permission. We do not access your contacts, SMS messages or call logs, and we do not collect location in the background.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • provide, operate and maintain the Website and Apps, including your account;
  • respond to your enquiries, send proposals and deliver the services you request;
  • send service messages such as confirmations, password resets, security alerts and notifications;
  • send marketing emails where you have agreed to them (you can unsubscribe at any time);
  • understand how the Services are used so we can improve them, and measure the results of our advertising;
  • answer your questions through the AI chat assistant on our Website, and provide AI-assisted features where an App or service offers them;
  • protect the Services against spam, fraud and abuse (for example, with reCAPTCHA); and
  • comply with our legal obligations and enforce our terms.

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR apply, we rely on these legal bases: performing a contract with you, our legitimate interests (for example, keeping the Services secure and improving them), your consent (for example, marketing, optional permissions and non-essential cookies) and compliance with legal obligations.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only in these situations:

  • Service providers that process data for us under contract and only on our instructions, including:
    • hosting and cloud infrastructure providers;
    • email delivery (Resend);
    • spam protection (Google reCAPTCHA);
    • analytics and advertising measurement (Google Analytics, Google Ads and Meta Pixel / Facebook Lead Ads);
    • app services such as Google Play services and Google Firebase (for example, for crash reporting, analytics and push notifications); and
    • AI service providers (such as Groq, and the AI models it runs) that power the Website chat assistant and other AI-assisted features. Chat messages are sent to these providers only to generate a reply.
  • Legal reasons – if required by law, regulation, legal process or a government request, or to protect the rights, property or safety of DNE Consultants, our users or others.
  • Business transfers – in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, with notice to you where required.
  • With your consent – in any other case where you have asked us to or agreed.

Third-party services have their own privacy policies, for example Google, Meta and Groq.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Website uses cookies and browser storage for essential functions (such as keeping you signed in and protecting forms), to remember preferences (such as light or dark mode), and for analytics and advertising measurement. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings.

5. Data Security

We protect your information with appropriate technical and organisational measures. All data sent between your browser or device and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS, passwords are stored only in hashed form, and access to personal data is limited to people who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you and the authorities of a data breach where the law requires it.

6. Data Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Account data – while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your data within 30 days.
  • Enquiries and leads – up to 3 years after our last contact with you, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • AI chat conversations – up to 12 months, or as part of an enquiry if you shared your contact details.
  • Logs and diagnostics – usually no longer than 12 months.
  • Records we must keep by law (for example, invoices and contracts) – for the period required by law.

Backups are overwritten on a rolling basis, normally within 90 days.

7. Deleting Your Account and Data

You can ask us to delete your account and the personal data associated with it at any time:

  • In the App – where an App has accounts, use the "Delete account" option in the App's settings or profile screen.
  • By email – send a request to hello@dneco.io with the subject "Account and data deletion request", the name of the App or service, and the email address or phone number linked to your account. You do not need to have the App installed.

We may ask you to confirm the request from the email address linked to your account. We will complete it within 30 days and confirm when it is done. We delete your profile, the content you uploaded and your usage data. We may keep limited information only where the law requires it (such as invoices) or to prevent fraud and abuse, and we delete it once it is no longer needed. You can also ask us to delete specific data without deleting your account.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • delete your information;
  • object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent at any time;
  • receive your information in a portable format; and
  • opt out of marketing emails, using the unsubscribe link in any email.

California residents also have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising their privacy rights. We do not sell or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising as defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@dneco.io. We will respond within 30 days (or sooner if the law requires). If you are in the EU, UK or another region with a data protection authority, you also have the right to complain to that authority.

9. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International Data Transfers

We are based in the United States, and our service providers may process information in other countries. When we transfer personal information across borders, we use appropriate safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses, where the law requires them.

DNECO Countdown

  • Platform: Android (package io.dneco.countdown)
  • Effective date: October 27, 2026

Backups. If you have turned on Android device backup, Android may include the app's data in your personal Google backup. This is controlled by you and Google; we cannot access it.

Deleting your data. Delete a countdown in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all of its data from your device. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Support: support@dneco.io
